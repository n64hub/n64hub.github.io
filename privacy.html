N64 Hub

Privacy

N64 Hub is a static site: no server of its own, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics. GitHub hosts it, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your ROM, saves and settings stay in this browser.

If you sign in with Google, your saves are copied to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The hub can see only that folder, none of your other files. It shows your name and email in its top bar and stores them nowhere.

Signing out revokes the hub's access. To delete the copied saves: Google Drive, Settings, Manage apps, N64 Hub, Delete hidden app data.

Questions: open an issue.
